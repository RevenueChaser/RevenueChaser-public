## Custom alerts page, in the bot's words (the part that matters for the migration)

- "Custom alerts are the alerts you build yourself on unusualwhales.com with your own filters."
- "A server with a premium server subscription can have them posted into a channel."
- Steps: create the alert in the alert settings on unusualwhales.com; "link your discord account to your UW account
  in your UW settings, as only alerts of the linked account can be selected"; run `/configure`, open Custom Alerts,
  subscribe an alert and pick the channel; the bot needs Manage Webhooks, "as custom alerts are only delivered
  through webhooks".
- "Only the server owner and users with Administrator permission can subscribe alerts, and a server subscription is
  needed, which /server_subscription sets up."

**So:** the `custom_alert` posts in `live-option-flow` and `congress-trades-fillings` are alerts of **one linked UW web
account**, delivered by webhook. If that account is avo's and his UW plan ends, those two feeds can stop even while
the server subscription runs. Whose account is linked is visible only in `/configure` (not opened).
