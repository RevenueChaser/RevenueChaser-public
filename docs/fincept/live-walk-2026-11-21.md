### Navigation behaviour [SEEN]
- The 15 top buttons switch the **screen**; each screen is a *document* in the dock manager.
- Opening a sub-screen (e.g. `RESEARCH` on a quote, `OPEN FULL SCREEN` on Data Sources) **does not replace** the
  current document — it opens a **second dock area side by side**, splitting the window 50/50, each with its own
  tab, its own `✕` and its own detach button.
- So the shell is a real MDI: several screens visible at once, each with an independent toolbar and status bar.
- `Navigate` menu groups: `Markets & Data`, `Trading & Portfolio`, `Crypto`, `Research & Intelligence`, `Tools`,
  `Docs`, `Support`, `About`. [SEEN] (submenu contents [NOT SEEN]: popups close when the capture takes focus)
